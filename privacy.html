Privacy Policy

The Midnight Appraiser · last updated October 25, 2026

The Midnight Appraiser is a single-player game. It does not have accounts, ads, analytics, or in-app purchases, and it does not collect, share, or sell personal information.

What the game stores

Your saved games and settings (such as brightness, text size, and sound) are stored on your own device, inside the app. They never leave your device. If you use the game's export feature to save a copy of a save file, that file goes wherever you choose to put it.

What the game does not do

The Android app does not need an internet connection to play and does not send any data anywhere. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files. The only permission it uses is vibration, for the game's haptic feedback; you can turn this off in the game's settings.

The web version

If you play the game in a browser, the same applies: saves stay in your browser's storage. The website is hosted by Netlify, which, like any web host, may keep ordinary server logs (such as your IP address and the time of the request) to run and secure the service. The game itself does not read or use them.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because everything is on your device, you can remove it at any time by deleting your saves in the game, clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the app.
