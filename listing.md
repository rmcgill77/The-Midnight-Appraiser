## Full description (4000 characters max)
You buy the uncanny by day and work out what it really is by night.

The Midnight Appraiser is a top-down pixel noir about running a pawn shop in a small town where some of what people sell you is not quite ordinary. Haggle across the counter, study each piece at your bench, decide what to put on your shelves, and try to keep the shop, the inspectors and the town on your side.

BY DAY
- Run your shop: haggle, read your customers, and decide what is worth buying.
- Walk the town: hardware store, market, the boardwalk, the beach, the chapel and its churchyard.
- Meet the townsfolk. Each has a story and a relationship that grows over time.
- Furnish your home and your shop with pieces you find and buy.

BY NIGHT
- Work at your bench and find out what a piece really is: inert, enchanted, or cursed.
- Walk the city after dark. The moon changes things. Something is watching, and the more you are seen, the worse it gets.
- Dig in the churchyard with Mike the gravekeeper, if you have the nerve.
- Fish and scavenge on the beach, and watch the tide come in.
- Open the shop after hours to a stranger crowd, if you dare. Inspectors are not always in uniform.

Plays fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases. Your saves stay on your device.
