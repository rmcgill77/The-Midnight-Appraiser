## Full description (4000 characters max)
You buy the uncanny by day and work out what it really is by night.

The Midnight Appraiser is a top-down pixel-art game about running a pawn shop in a small, strange city where some of what people sell you is not quite ordinary. Haggle across the counter, study each piece at your bench, decide what goes on your shelves, and try to keep the shop, the inspectors and your own sleep schedule intact.

A CITY THAT KEEPS TIME
- The day runs on a real clock, from morning light through dusk and into the dark. Lamps and windows come on, shadows move, and the seasons change how the streets look.
- Fronts, fog, thunder, snow and wind roll through, and the ground gets wet and white with them. Check the forecast before you plan your week.
- Townsfolk have their own routines and are in different places at different times of day.

BY DAY
- You choose your shop's hours, up to eight a day, with a day off each week. Open the shop from its sign and see who walks in.
- Haggle, read your customers, and work out what is worth buying.
- Take appraisal jobs that can run for days, and manage your errand hours.
- Meet the townsfolk. Each has a story, a birthday, and a friendship that grows over time.
- Make your character: a big creator with pronouns, many looks and a dozen portrait moods.

BY NIGHT
- Work at your basement bench and find out what a piece really is: inert, enchanted or cursed. A UV lamp shows what is hidden.
- Step out after dark and the night run begins. Enter places like the laundromat, the subway line and the parking garage by their own doors. Something is watching, and the more you are seen, the worse it gets.
- Open the shop late to a stranger crowd, if you dare. Inspectors are not always in uniform.

PRESSURE
- Rent is due. Heat builds from dangerous goods. A seasonal audit from the Municipal Office of Anomalous Property is coming.
- Skip sleep and it shows: you walk slower, make mistakes at the counter, your screen starts to swim, and eventually you lose a day. Your friends notice.

Plays fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases. Your saves stay on your device.
